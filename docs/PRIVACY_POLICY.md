# AC Remote: Repair & Diagnose Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026

Published policy: <https://smartuniversalacremote.blogspot.com/2026/08/blog-post.html>

AC Remote: Repair & Diagnose is designed to control compatible air conditioners on the same local network as the user's device.

## Information stored on the device

AC Remote: Repair & Diagnose stores the following information locally so the app can retain the user's setup:

- Device names, rooms, brands, models, local IP addresses, and ports
- Climate settings and schedules
- Theme, temperature unit, haptic, and connection preferences
- Onboarding completion status
- Whether the one-time remote-access reward has been completed

This information is not sold. Depending on operating-system settings, locally stored app data may be included in encrypted device backups.

## Advertising and consent

AC Remote: Repair & Diagnose uses Google AdMob to display advertising. Subject to the user's region, device settings, and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, general device information, ad interactions, and diagnostics to deliver, limit, secure, and measure ads.

AC Remote: Repair & Diagnose uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent where required before requesting ads. When required, users can revisit their choices through **Settings > Ad privacy choices**. Google processes advertising data under its own privacy policies and retention practices.

On iOS 14 and later, AC Remote: Repair & Diagnose also uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before allowing access to the device advertising identifier. If permission is declined, the app can continue to show ads, but the identifier is not included for cross-app tracking or advertising measurement.

The production publisher must configure the required consent messages in the AdMob Privacy & messaging console and keep the public version of this policy aligned with the final AdMob and mediation configuration.

## Local network access

AC Remote: Repair & Diagnose requests local network access to discover and communicate with compatible air conditioners. Local network addresses are used for device communication and are not intended to be transmitted to AC Remote: Repair & Diagnose servers.

## Data retention and deletion

Saved devices and schedules remain on the device until the user removes them or uninstalls the app. Users can remove individual devices from Device Details. Uninstalling AC Remote: Repair & Diagnose removes its locally stored application data, subject to operating-system backup behavior.

## Children's privacy

AC Remote: Repair & Diagnose is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as AC Remote: Repair & Diagnose's features change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.
